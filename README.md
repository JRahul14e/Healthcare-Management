The Unified Healthcare Management System is a comprehensive web-based platform designed to seamlessly integrate hospital, donor, and patient data, streamlining the processes of organ and blood donation, patient matching, inventory management, and request handling. Healthcare plays a vital role in ensuring the well-being of individuals, and the effective coordination between hospitals, donors, and patients significantly enhances the quality of care. Historically, medical records and donor-patient coordination were handled manually using paperwork, telephonic communication, and fragmented databases, which often led to delays, data inconsistencies, and limited accessibility. Traditional systems lacked centralized storage, real-time updates, and automated donor-patient matching, causing inefficiencies in critical medical situations. Earlier solutions relied on physical record-keeping and local hospital databases that were not interconnected, making it difficult to access donor availability or match organ compatibility promptly. The motivation behind developing a unified solution stems from the urgent need to address delays in donor matching, untracked inventory, and manual errors in the current healthcare infrastructure. Rapid technological advancements and increased reliance on digital healthcare services inspired the creation of a centralized, automated platform to overcome the existing limitations. Problems such as lack of real-time donor data, inefficient matching processes, unorganized medical inventories, and communication gaps have often resulted in lost opportunities to save lives. The proposed system offers an efficient, web-based solution that enables role-based access for patients, donors, and hospital staff, facilitates registration, tracks blood and organ availability, manages donation and request workflows, and ensures secure, timely matching of donors and patients. Integrated alerts and approval mechanisms further enhance the decision-making process, while a user-friendly interface improves accessibility and adoption across healthcare providers. By centralizing data and introducing automation, this platform significantly reduces delays and enhances overall medical responsiveness and transparency.
